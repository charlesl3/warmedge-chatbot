Caroline Zhang has gorgeous spirals that always got +GOE due to her flexibility, extension and control. Her body proportions (thicker build, longer torso and shorter legs) are very different from Sasha Cohen's, but I don't find that that interferes with my--or the judges'--perception of her spiral positions. She just could have used more speed. https://youtu.be/v4WQCdTHUwg?t=26s

And now, back to the original question. . . I favor Sasha Cohen's spirals over Shizuka's, due to Sasha's ability to present her spirals with such intensity and impact. This is why I'd give Sasha a slight edge over Mao as well. Michelle Kwan presented hers with impact but her positions themselves were not that impressive. I would crown Yulia Lipnitskaia as the post-Sasha generation spiral queen. Her positions remind me of Sasha's.

Speaking from the perspective of someone who used to know nothing about figure skating, I quite like Yuna's spirals. When I saw the Gershwin program for the first time, the placement of the spiral made me emotional. Unlike other skaters with limited flexibility, Yuna has great control of her spirals, solid speed and edge and they are well timed with the music. https://youtu.be/qgNTjcF9ngA?t=3m10s, https://youtu.be/Vh-850kqJoE?t=50s

Can't belive no one mentioned Ashley yet. She has one the best spirals in current field. Her Charlotte is not perfect, but she is the only one who goes for that and I love it. Such as I love the spirals in Black Swan. https://youtu.be/Kq_Fgh4Qwb4?t=2m37s, https://youtu.be/Iz-gVGsYNrA?t=4m36s

Sasha is just perfection. No comment needed. https://youtu.be/ELFerA2QZX8?t=2m21s

Honorary mention: Mirai. She has great flexibility and her spirals looks great. https://youtu.be/3rJX0_QqCTE?t=2m12s

Why is that my loss? Tonya was not graceful. I personally never enjoyed her skating. She looked heavy to me on the ice and that, for me, detracted from the gracefulness of her skating. That's not to say she wasn't athletic.

Seriously I don't understand what the issue here is other than someone or some are miffed because some of us prefer a figure skater that is lithe and graceful. There are skaters that are not lithe and graceful and they still win medals. The argument that Johnny Weir is Johnny Weir also makes no sense because he was trying to stay trim. But the fact that someone with a certain body type is able to rotate faster and tighter is sort of fact, isn't it?

Back to the thread topic - I definitely prefer someone that is flexible, has long lines, points their toe in a spiral, and looks like they're in control and not about to lose their balance. And I absolutely do not feel that that opinion is foolish or harmful. It's a personal choice and seemingly the personal choice of many, many coaches.
