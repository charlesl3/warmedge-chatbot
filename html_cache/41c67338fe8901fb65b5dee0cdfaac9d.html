Carolina Moscheni and Ádám Lukács have split AGAIN, just 6 months after they got back together after splitting a year ago.

Judging by Carolina's statement on Facebook, I don't think they will be giving it another go:

Hi everyone, I would like just to take a minute to announce that Adam and I will not skate together anymore. It has been a tough period for both of us and we felt we could have made it together like we did in the past together too. But at the end of the day I feel that respect and love for what we do should come first. We are first human beings before skaters. A lack of this understanding and some unpleasant events made me make my choice and sadly put an end to our partnership after the many chances I tried to give it. My love for this sport is still stronger than ever and I couldn't be more grateful to be part of this amazing world. Thanks everyone who helped our journey. I am definitely going to be looking for a new partner who is willing to work hard and share the love for ice dance with me.

Source: https://www.facebook.com/carolina.moscheni/posts/10207331085500929​

Oh well! I wish them both all the best for the future.
