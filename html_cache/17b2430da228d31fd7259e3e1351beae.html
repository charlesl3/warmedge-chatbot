Hey everyone. First post! I hope this is the right board for this question.

I'm a 19 year old girl. Completely non athletic, and never really have been. I did do figure skating til about 12-13, not competitive though, and then I dropped it, though I do have alright skating skills, very comfortable on the ice. I've always been a HUGE fan of figure skating though, probably my favourite thing in the world to watch.

I'm taking some time off school to explore some other options and I'll have quite a bit of time. And I was wondering whether to start figure skating again. Thing is, if I start figure skating, I'm want to succeed at it, no matter what. Regardless of the amount of work it would take, how crazy would it be to start figure skating again at this age, in hopes of going competitive? Like, extremely competitive, all the way to the top? I've heard people worry about starting too late at 14 or 15, let alone my age. I have the time and patience and dedication, I'm just not sure if I'm physically viable, seeing how skaters peak and retire so young.

Thanks for reading in advance!

Even if you were 9 (or younger) rather than 19, extremely athletic, and had the capability to skate a couple hours every day for the next 10 years, the chances of becoming "extremely competitive, all the way to the top" would be very slim.

Without all those advantages, it's just not a realistic goal.

With the advantages you do have (time, patience, and dedication), and assuming that also includes enough ice time available near you and enough money to practice and take lessons, you could learn enough skills to succeed at accomplishing realistic goals. Start with some modest short-term goals and adjust your longer term goals as you accomplish the earlier ones.

What would you be most interested in? Passing tests? Performing in shows? Competing lower level local competitions and someday maybe mid-level competitions? Singles skating with jumps and spins? Ice dancing? Synchronized skating? Depending what's available where you live and how much time and money you have to devote, you could find modest success you could be proud of in any of those areas.

If you enjoy skating, spend some time on the ice, sign up for lessons, get to know other skaters, and check out what your options are now and what they might be after a few years of training.

But if you go into it thinking that anything less than international competition is failure, then you will be disappointed.

Welcome! First off, read through this very well written thread:

https://www.goldenskate.com/forum/showthread.php?62596-So-you-want-to-be-a-figure-skater

i couldn't really start skating regularly until I was 19. Back then there were not many competitions for "older" beginners. Now it's different. If you have an ISI associated rink you can compete in their competitions or if your rink is part of US Figure Skating there are categories in many competitions for "young adults". And more facets too: Solo ice dance, showcase competitions, interpretive events, etc. There are even international Adult competitions.

Give it try, make friends, have fun and see where your skating takes you.
