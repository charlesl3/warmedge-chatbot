Check out the Vitamin String Quartet. They do covers of Metallica and other bands. They did one of Fade to Black, but listening to that, I don't think it's "skateable". They've done a ton of Metallica, though. Apocalyptica also does a lot of metal and etc. covers.

For tempo for skating at your level (based on my own, which is adult pre-bronze for freestyle), think this way: Metallica "Nothing Else Matters" = probably too slow. Metallica "Enter Sandman" - probably too fast. So something in between those two on the Metallica scale. But in terms of what's "skate able", Nothing Else Matters would work. I just suspect it's too slow for what your coach is after, based on what you said about theSaint Saens piece, but you can try it on her.

Gee, I thought I was hip because Gorillaz is one of my favorite bands – LOL! Never heard of Opeth, Amorphis or Angra but I did give a listen and expanded my music horizons.
Here's my .02 cents – you're a beginning competitor so don't pick one that is above your skating level and speed.

The Opeth song is good choice.
The Angra song is a good choice too – you could easily have some choreography and interpretation in that music!

The Amorphis song is a little rough and it sounds like it's telling a story which you're not likely to get across in 1 minute.

I'm not a Janis fan-I find her voice very, very grating, especially in that version. BUT - it is YOUR music so if you can "feel it" it's an option.

My comment on Fade to Black is how will you chose 1 minute out of 7? I'd save that for when you progress to a longer program time.

The classical selections you mentioned are all good choices.

And as other posters have mentioned, there are bands that do covers and instrumentals of rock music that you could check out.
Let us know what you pick!!!

Picking music for a program is much easier when you have been skating for several years, only because when you have more things that can go in a program, you can picture yourself doing them when hearing music.

Here is something crazy. I'm absolutely a non-fan of Ozzy, but one day the piece "Iron Man" came on the radio and I was actually shocked that suddenly while it was playing, the music was combining in my head with bits of choreography! Then I had to go on You Tube to listen to it, and notes certain segments that could be cut easily to make a program shorter than the entire piece. I just recently started really skating again, so it's not like I have a program to do, but this is the sort of thing that happens in the heads of skaters.
