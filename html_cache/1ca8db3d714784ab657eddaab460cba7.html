I can't answer the thermal tight question because I don't wear them.

For the OTB tights - you still wear your Mondor knee highs or whatever socks you wear normally. You put on your skates as normal.
Then the boot part of the OTB tights goes over the boot after you've laced them up. I'm not sure if those OTB tights have any type of attachment that go under the skates to actually keep them over the boot and stop them from riding up.

I'm going to join the others and suggest going with either regular footed tights or tights / leggings that just end up going over the top part of the skate hooks (you'd still wear your knee highs under the latter option).

OTB tights are going to get holes in them and start to look bad if they are worn consistently.
If you're thinking that you want to wear OTB tights to keep your skates looking new-ish then I suggest getting separate boot covers for them. Those go on before you put on your skates and then you pull them over your boots after you've laced them up. They should have elastic at the ankle and at the bottom to keep them in place.

Happy shopping!

For otbt check how they stay down. Some have hook and eye, others plastic latches, and others Velcro. Of all of them velcro is the easiest to use but hardest to keep the Velcro off the tights when cleaning them. Some clearance ones still have the hook and eye you have to sew on yourself. People who get really cold use in the boot tights and then otbt over them. When my daughter uses them, she wears mondor socks and folds the tops over the hooks on her boots. She does have knit ankle tights, she wore them the most often when she was really young. Mondor makes them. She tends to run hot so she doesn't often use them.
