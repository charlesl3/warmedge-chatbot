For reference i’m 5’4 and 125lbs and i have african/ egyptian feet and regular arches.

At the time i was working on my waltz jump but now im working on my loop, flip and lutz as well as my sit, camel and backspin. I never ended up upgrading my boots as my coach assured me they would be fine but i skate an hour everyday and i have thick creases on my skates and i have to re tie them every 30 minutes or so because my foot is slipping and today my foot almost fully slipped out of my boot while i was doing a flip.

Howdy! I am in DFW as well.

The only options you have here is Blades by Todd in North Richland Hills, but many people also venture as far as to Patty at Houston Skate and Sport Orthotics Center.

Were you professionally fitted before?

I ask, because your size could change, if your size is not at Blades by Todd, they require full payment up front prior to them ordering the correct size for you and having you physically try them on. Getting fitted is not free if you do not purchase from them, so have money available for that as well. They will still credit the cost towards your new pair of skates when you decide to purchase from them in the future.

If you have been professionally fitted before, then you can call both Blades by Todd and Houston Skate and Sport Orthotics Center (I do not know her stock or policies) in advance and ask if your size is in stock in other models, especially if you like Jacksons in particular.

For coaching, are you ISI or USFS? What are your goals? Are you a Dallas Figure Skating Club member or are you interested to learn more? We have a show that is open to all Dallas Figure Skating Club members in December in Grapevine.

On October 8 we will learn who our rink representatives are, and I'll come back and let you know who yours is so you can have a hyper-local resource regarding figure skating and the club.
