Newbiespectator said:

Click to expand...

The slow-motion replays at the end of programs definitely tend to be the best to use for this sort of thing (although whether that is 'high quality' or not depends on the source). The replays usually show close-ups that weren't available during the program, and the jumps already being in slow-motion makes it easier to edit. It all depends on the elements you are trying to highlight and which camera angles are available of that specific element (not all are shown during the post-program slow-motion replays).

For the sake of comparison, I used the same NBC clip posted earlier in this topic of Joshua's 3A at real-time speed (which isn't the best quality out there of this program but is still pretty great) and tried to see what I could come up with.

From Joshua's FS at 2015 Nationals, the 3A in slow-motion, then super-slow-motion, then reverse slow-motion, and finally normal speed:
