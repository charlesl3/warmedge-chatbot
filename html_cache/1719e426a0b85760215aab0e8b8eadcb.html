I'm posting this reply here because I wanted to ask a question, and I thought it would sound insensitive if I was to ask it in one of the other threads that you announced the split in.

What ever happened to the girl that Ryan Dodds competed in Pairs with, Jessica Rotondo? Is she still skating? And, if so, is she available?

Although it is a good while since Jessica and Ryan competed internationally, Jessica is actually a year younger than Katia. So, assuming that she is no longer skating with Ryan, she could be an option for Harley.

The last time that Katia and Harley competed internationally was in October. So, if he was looking to be back as quickly as possible, a partner from overseas would need to have last competed before that.

But, of course, this is Pairs. It takes time for partners to gel and get comfortable with each other. And if Harley and his new partner want to compete at the Beijing Olympics in 2 years from now, time is not a luxury they would have if this partner does not already have an Australian passport. It took Katia a few years to get Australian citizenship.

So, that was why I was thinking of looking within Australia. And for a girl that already had Pairs experience.
