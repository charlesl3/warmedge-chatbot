Well, I don't know, but I think it's possible that people thought or assumed that Maxime was just helping Deanna out with certain pairs elements, getting a different set of coaches' perspectives, and so forth. And that Kyueun, and other people in the rink, didn't really think that Deanna and Maxime were trying out together.

When Meagan Duhamel was on TSL recently, Dave quoted Deanna as saying that Meagan had, for one instance, helped her with the death spiral. Meagan explained that it's really hard (well, she'd never seen it happen) for a team to get level 4 on a ds when the woman's back is arched very high. Which is how Deanna had always performed any ds -- I've always noticed how beautiful it made her posture. But in this points system, it isn't the way to maximize. Which I was very interested to hear, because I'd wondered why more pairs women didn't arch their backs more.

Anyway, I think it's conceivable that some assumed that Deanna just needed a partner to practice with. I think it's been done with other pairs in the past, and didn't necessarily mean a tryout. I'm not picking an argument; I'm just imagining how it might have come to be that Kyueun didn't know what was going on and then felt blind-sided. But imagining isn't knowing.
