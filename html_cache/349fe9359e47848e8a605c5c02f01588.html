hi and welcome! Have you gotten a pro fitting from a figure skating boot expert? Because you really need to do that if you haven't. They'll measure an trace your foot and suggest what brands work best with your feet. Be aware: Figure skating is a pricey sport.

If you do truly fit in Jackson the best the ones suggested to you and the ones you are asking about are not going to work for what you want and need. Being an adult and such, the lowest stiffness and style of boot you could safely go with in Jackson's line up would be Debut Fusion (this is boot only) you'd best pair it with an intermediate blade either MK Pro or JW Coronation Ace. Jackson Premiere Fusion would be next boot up.

Honestly, we don't know what your foot shape and type is, or some other things that necessary for proper boot suggestions/fitting.
Also, if you let us know your whereabouts in the world, we can suggest a good proper figure skate fitter in your area or nearby.

Good luck and if you need anything else, just ask!

It's worth talking to the shop about your budget and seeing what they might be able to do for you! Jackson has made quite a few updates to their boot lines in recent years, so a Jackson dealer might be able to find you some "new old stock" boots (meaning brand new, unused models that have been discontinued).

I was able to save quite a bit of money on my skates this way - I have the pre-Fusion men's Debut with the traditional cork/leather sole, and MK Pros with K-picks (which are small extra picks welded onto the side of the blade next to the main pick - the idea was that they'd provide extra grip and stability for toe jumps, but most skaters found they didn't make much difference and they were quietly discontinued). My fitter got me the boots for $275 (the MSRP for the Debut Fusion is $370, I believe), and I found the blades on eBay being sold by a skate shop in Chicago for $100 (MK Pros are usually $220 brand new).

(I should note that I got extremely lucky with the blades - buying on eBay is risky since you can't really be sure the seller is accurately describing the condition of the blades, but in this case the seller was honest, and the blades were indeed brand new in the box when I got them. If I had the option of getting new old stock blades through a shop versus on eBay, I'd go with the shop every time. And also worth mentioning - don't buy used boots or blades, especially online. It's almost impossible to tell before buying if they're in good enough condition to use safely, and it's not worth risking injury by trying to save money.)

The Debut update was several years ago at this point, so I'd guess it might be hard to find the pre-Fusion version now - but there was another update within the last couple years where they added the cut-resistant Duraguard material to the front of the boot, so the original Debut Fusion might still be kicking around. They've made a few updates to the Premiere in the last few years as well - so worth asking about that model as well.

Hope this is helpful!
