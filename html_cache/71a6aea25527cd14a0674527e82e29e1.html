International Dances

This is a list of the current US Figure Skating International Dances, 10 in all:
Cha Cha Congelado
Silver Samba
Rhumba
Midnight Blues
Austrian Waltz
Ravensburger Waltz
Tango Romantica
Yankee Polka
Golden Waltz
Finnstep

I am listing them in some order of difficulty. We have a number of skaters who test these dances in our area, and 3-4 male dance coaches and partners who take the female dancers through the tests. My opinion is that the first 4 dances on this list seem to be the easiest for skaters to learn, followed by the 2 waltzes and Tango Ro (that require great timing and wonderful twizzles). The last 3 tend to be more challenging due to the difficulty of both steps and timing. Of course, this all varies some based on the individual skater's dance skills. Keep in mind that these dances are not designed to be skated as solo dances, and that also makes them more challenging. The Midnight Blues, added in 2002, and the Finnstep are the newest of the dances, and all the ice dance coaches had to learn these dances in order to teach and skate them.

You don't have to pass a test to take someone else through a test. I've never tested with a coach and tested all my dances with my same-level partner from the start. However, they can't test at the same time. When you test, the whole panel needs to be watching the skater who is the tester. They can do it twice back to back, with one person testing each time.
