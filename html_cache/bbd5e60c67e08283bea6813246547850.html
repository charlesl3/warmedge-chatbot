If the lining is suede like mine were, it might get better with another few sessions when the suede smooths out at the points of friction. Redoing heat-moulding if applicable might help too to make more room for your malleoli. I know people with customs who still needed punchouts for their various bony protrusions (bunions and other usual skaters' battle scars).

Also if that pad is a donut, replacing it with a non-cutout pad might help?
