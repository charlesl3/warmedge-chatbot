Hi! Welcome to Australia! Welcome to skating in Australia!

No such system as ISU, I think you mean ISA? With a 2S/2T you have clearly long since levelled out of Aussie Skate. If you're talking to the rinks about what your level was in your previous country they may just be confused. What test level are you where you were from?

It may be worth getting in contact with the ISA to see what you would have to do to convert your tests. In Australia Adult levels are based on which ISA test you've passed (Prelim for Bronze, Elementary patterns for Silver, etc, etc). You can certainly choose to skate Adult if you want to compete, but you may be able to choose standard levels as well if you want.

Regarding lessons, you should just enquire straight up about private lessons at the various rinks. While I don't know a huge amount about the rinks in Sydney I'm guessing that finding group lessons for an adult with doubles is going to be impossible. Most adult "group lessons" are coffee skate type arrangements. Pick the rink easiest for you to get to - that may be closest to your work place or your home or even en route.

No way are you too old for Intermediate! I competed in Intermediate for a couple of years, I was 15 (and stayed in Intermediate until I stopped competing at age 17) but there were quite a few older skaters that I competed against. One girl was competing in that level until she was 25-27ish. Intermediate is definitely better to be in than Basic Novice A or B, they are mostly full of younger skaters who are very competitive (and very good - jumping and skating clean is obviously much easier when you're young, both physically and mentally). Intermediate is still quite competitive, and has some very good skaters preparing to move up to Novice, but you won't be competing against little kids.

Having said that though, why don't you want to do adult? The adult track is perfect for those who do things other than skating and don't want to take competing suuuuuuper seriously, but still want some friendly competition and a way of keeping motivated and having goals to achieve. You can take it as seriously as you want, but won't have to deal with the really competitive atmosphere that is often there in the "standard" ISA levels. And tbh, the stigma against adult skaters is quite unreasonable - some are quite good! A lot of adults in Gold and above are doing axels/doubles.

I kind of understand where you're coming from, as when I used to compete, my thoughts were something along the lines of "I'll never compete in adult, that would be 'giving in'!", but honestly, now I really want to compete in the adult levels. I've moved on from wanting to be a competitive skater (I'm 21 now, haven't competed in 4 years but I still skate for fun), but I would love to go back to competing for enjoyment and to keep motivated, without the pressure that I used to feel when competing in the standard levels (I keep saying I will, but studying and life keep getting in the way ). Plus, (almost) everyone in the adult skating community is so, so nice! It's almost completely devoid of the high school cattiness that there sometimes is in skating.

Sorry for rambling a bit! tl;dr is do adult track! Adult track is fun!
