There is no need to pay too much for a dress, especially if it's for basic generic use, such as testing or a simple programt. A reasonable ballpark is about the same cost as any other piece of garment of decent quality. To keep costs down, consider buying second-hand. Many clubs hold used dress sales, and some skating retail shops also sell used dresses. You can also look at online retail sites like ebay or etsy for affordable new dresses. Several dress makers run their business through these online platforms, and make dresses to order.

BTW, often the reason why some dresses are so expensive is because of the materials used and labor:

- expensive fabrics, such as lace fabrics or rare fabric patterns
- sparkles (usually Swarovski crystals) are expensive to purchase and even more expensive to pay for labor to apply onto the dress. But sparkles is what makes a dress... sparkle! Almost every skater I know loooooves sparkles, tons of sparkles, so I get that they are enticing. You can reduce labor cost by applying the sparkles yourself, if you know how.

Otherwise, a basic dress is just essentially spandex and a bit of labor to sew it together. Considering that it's a first dress for a beginner, I can't imagine why you'll need a very specialized designed dress with expensive fabric and tons of sparkles.

If a person has a standard figure and isn't picky, buying a dress off of eBay or secondhand, would probably be cheaper and less risky.

I typically spend around 100. Carrie Jone's often has a sale or ebay and those I go up to about 150.

But it totally depends on the needs - my daughter was in a show and needed about 5 costumes, I think my total spent was under $200. She needed items with a big visual impact, so I could get ideas from dance.

For a serious event, I am closer to the 150 or I buy a used dress that works - again closer to 100 and I add stones or make repairs.

Testing for moves in the field was the least expesive - you don't need stones or even a dress. For free skate she wore the same dress she wore to do the program in a compeition. When she was a kid, it was easy to borrow or rent or buy like new used dresses. But as an adult sized skater, most of her friends have left the sport so it is a little harder.
