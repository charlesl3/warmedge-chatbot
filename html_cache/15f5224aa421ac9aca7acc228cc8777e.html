Both the Pattern 99 and Gold Seal have an overall 8' rocker radius but they have very different rocker profiles. The rocker profile is the shape of the rocker in the front half of the blade. You want to stick with a blade that has a rocker profile similar to what you are used to. Why? Because you are going to be working on the double axel and if you have a blade with a different rocker profile, it's going to change the timing and feel of your axel takeoff and mess you up. In fact, it will change the timing and feel of all of your edge takeoffs (salchow, axel and loop).

What is the exact model of blade you are using now? If it's the Supreme, then it copies the Gold Seal rocker profile and you should get Gold Seal blades. If it's the Elite, then it copies the Pattern 99 rocker profile and you should get Pattern 99s. If it's the Freestyle, then it's a copy of the Phantom, which has a rocker profile more like Pattern 99. If it's a Legacy blade, the rocker profile is a little closer to the Gold Seal, so you should probably go with Gold Seal. P.S. I know the Pattern 99 has a LOWER stanchion than other blades, but the Gold Seal is not higher than other blades. I went from MK Professional to MK Gold Star to Wilson Gold Seal and the height of the blades was the same. The Gold Seal is an excellent spinning blade because it has a rocker profile that is very full and round right behind the bottom pick. The Pattern 99 has a more gradual spinning rocker, i.e., a little less round and a little farther back.

Yes, the Phantom blade has a 7' rocker, but the rocker profile is similar to the Pattern 99. The overall rocker radius (7' vs 8') will be felt more in the back half of the blade, while the rocker profile will be felt more in the front half of the blade. The MK Gold Star and Wilson Gold Seal have a very similar rocker profile, but different rocker radius. Same is true of Phantom and Pattern 99.
