For mucho years, I've gotten by without special socks for skating. I simply used Gold Toe casual dress socks: thin, absorbent (high-percentage cotton), and durable. In particular, the seams were finely stitched. As with many products these days, "they don't make them like they used to". The latest batches have coarsely stitched seams, especially around the toe cap. Some have pronounced nubs, causing pressure and pain when skating (OK in regular shoes). I was hoping the nubs would soften with repeated washings, but they've actually gotten harder. This doesn't appear to be an isolated problem; reviews of Gold Toes indicate the quality has really deteriorated.

So, guys, what socks do you wear with figure skates? I would like to avoid >50% nylon, and would prefer >80% cotton. But the main thing is I want well-crafted seams that don't bunch up and don't dig into me.

Thanks.

Thanks everyone for all your suggestions.

(1) High-percentage nylon socks just won't cut it for me. I realize the thinnest high-percentage cotton socks are thicker than the thinnest high-percentage nylon socks, but they are still reasonably thin, and I don't need ultra-thin.

(2) The Thinees seem to have good reviews and appear to be popular in Canada. I couldn't find US retailers. They are reasonably priced in Canada, but one seller on Amazon US wants a whopping ~US$28 for one pair. The material of both Thinees and the Edeas is listed as 97% polyamide. I looked that up. That term covers a variety of materials, including nylon. So I'm not sure whether they are made of a better material, or whether the marketing guys just wanted a more high-tech name for nylon.

(3) Thanks Sk8DSmom for the tip on diabetic socks. Never heard of them before. I'll definitely look into them. But I'll need to check carefully. Amazon has an extensive selection, and the quality range, not surprisingly, is all over the map, according to reviews.
