Wow, there's 8 new competitions, 8 competitions are back and compared to last season only one disappeared from the calendar (the Alpen Trophy)
We've rarely seen a so big change between 2 seasons.

- 4 new events in Asia with a "new" Challenger Series event in Taiwan. The South-East Asian Games are back in Philippines and Kazakhstan will hold their first 100% figure skating event in the memory of Denis Ten. Shanghai Trophy is back too after a first edition in 2017.
- 3 new events in Hungary, Budapest Trophy and Valentine Cup. Avas Cup is back... We'll soon see one competition per week in this country
- 3 new events in the Balkan Countries : Black Sea Cup (Bulgaria), Bellu Memorial (Romania) and Sarajevo Open back in Bosnia-Hercegovina.
- 2 new events in Italy : Icelab Cup and the comeback of the Mezzaluna Cup, which last edition was held in 2000!
- 3 events are back in Europe : Ice Challenge (Austria), Coupe de Nice (France, with new name and sponsor) and Golden Lynx (Belarus, was already held as a JGP)
- 1 brand new event in UK : the Tayside Trophy

Changes to Philadelphia Summer International Competition for 2019/20:

In 2018/19, only Senior Ladies and Senior Men competed.

In 2019/20, Junior Ladies and Junior Men are returning to PSIC competition after a one-year hiatus.
(In 2017/18, Ting Cui and Ryan Dunk were PSIC's Junior winners.
Segments for Juniors were not included in the Announcement for 2018/19, and did not take place in 2018/19.)​

No plan to attempt Pairs competition in 2019/20.
(Senior Pairs originally was included in the Announcement for 2018/19, but did not materialize.)​
