If you have a particular skater that you are interested in seeing a particular jump in slow motion/backward/paused/multiple angles if available/ etc.... please feel free to ask. If you could provide a link that would be very helpful (720p quality and up are worth finding) and the approximate time that the jump occurs in the program. I can't promise to do every video suggested but I'll do my best. That I will promise.

Here is the video that I posted the other day so you can reference the type of editing I can do. Feel free to add suggestions(I usually add music ) and also understand that some angles are just not good enough to determine edges/rotations/height all of the time.

Obviously I'm not the only person with a video editor so if anyone else has interest in posting their own videos in this thread then have at it

When I first made the Satoko video I expected people to attack my fandom of Yulia and call her out because she has edge issues and was getting edge calls all season. So I went and made a video of her 3f from CoC this season and was surprised to see that the jump she was getting called on this season was one of the better examples to watch from a slow motion point of view of a 3f. IMO she is on the correct edge and gets full rotation. The only issue is at take off she gets to just short of 180 and sort of jerks her toe but I think it's actually a clean jump and it seems most everyone when viewed at this speed has these issues. Believe me....I wasn't expecting to find her 3f with this quality. Especially for her being such a small jumper. IMHO she executes all the qualities a judge should look for in a +1GOE jump and maybe +2 if you stretch a bit as some judges do based on circumstances and consider the set up and transition out into Choreography.
