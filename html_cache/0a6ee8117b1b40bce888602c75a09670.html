Quad Watch (Ladies)

ever since Alexandra Trusova’s history-making free skate at the 2018 Junior Worlds the quadruple trend has really caught on. This thread here is dedicated to keeping track of various quad attempts from all ladies (senior and junior) and analyzing them.

So far there have been 6 ladies who have attempted quads in actual competition:

- Alexandra Trusova
- Anna Shcherbakova
- Alysa Liu
- Elizabet Tursynbaeva
- Miki Ando
- Sasha Cohen

out of these 6, four (Trusova, Tursynbaeva, Ando and Shcherbakova) have landed and rotated (according to protocols) their quads, one (Liu) rotated hers.

videos of 13 (?) ladies attempting quads have surfaced and a total of 17 (?) have been rumored to be doing or trying them. The nine include:

- Alexandra Trusova
- Anna Shcherbakova
- Alysa Liu
- Elizabet Tursynbaeva
- Miki Ando
- Sasha Cohen
- You Young
- Elizaveta Berstovskaya (UR)
- Anastasia Tarakanova (fall, UR)
- Yelim Kim
- Rika Kihira
- Anita Ostlund (slight UR)
- Ekaterina Kurakova

others rumored to have been trying quads are:

- Anna Tarusina
- Maria Sotskova
- Evgenia Medvedeva
- Alina Zagitova

feel free to add on!
