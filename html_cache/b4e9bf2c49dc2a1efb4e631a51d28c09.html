Hello,
I have a question about the healing time of torn ankle ligaments. I had a crash with another skater during warm-up and took a bad fall where my left ankle (landing leg) got stuck underneath my upper leg and butt. Luckily I didn't break any bones but tore 2 of 3 outer ankle ligaments. This was 4 days ago...
Has anyone dealt with something similiar? How long did you had to pause skating? The doctor said I will be fine to skate again in 6 weeks if I wear my ankle brace daily and nightly til then. I also will have physical therapy as soon as the swelling goes down.
I find it hard to believe that this will be over in only 6 weeks as at the moment I cannot put any weight on my ankle at all.

Sorry to hear of your injury Get well soon!

Ankle sprain recovery times vary case-by-case, anything from a couple of weeks to a few months depending on how extensive the damage is and which ligaments are affected. Listen to your doctor/PT on this - they should know best from examining your injury how long it takes and what you should or shouldn't be doing.

That said, just to give a general idea of what might be expected for a moderate sprain: I sprained my ankle syndesmotic ligaments twice (these are generally slower to heal than outer/lateral ligament sprains). I had crutches/rigid boot during the first week or so, two months off ice in a brace doing PT, then another month of basic skating skills without jumps. Recovery wasn't a linear process; sometimes I was pleasantly surprised at how well I was doing and other times frustrated at the lingering pain and weakness. Ankles are fine now but I still have a bit of mental block doing the elements I was injured on. Try to get good medical advice and be patient Good luck!

If it's completely torn (grade 3) you might be looking at a timeframe closer to mine or longer. You might be cleared to step on the ice in a few weeks but it takes longer than that to regain the strength and skills for competition especially if you're doing harder doubles/triples.
I don't skate at that level, but even counter-twisting turns like brackets and choctaws hurt for the first 2-3 weeks I was back.

Just a little update... Since the swelling and bruising was so bad and wouldnt get down and my ankle just felt really strange (totally different to my former experience with torn ligaments), I was sent to get an MRI.
Turned out that I also ruptured the anterior part of the syndesmosis (AIFTL) and got a micro fracture in my fibula. Luckily both classified as stable so I was able to get conservative treatment instead of surgery (they took the time to evaluate pros and cons of both treatments and let me chose).
But this means that I am still walking on crutches with limited weight bearing and in a moon boot for another two weeks, followed by rehab and intense strenghtening and that I will not return to the ice for another 3-4 months.
So if your reading this post because you have similar problems, listen to your body. If anything feels strange or gets worse with initial treatment, let it get checked again.
