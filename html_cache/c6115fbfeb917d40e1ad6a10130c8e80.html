Moxiejan said:
This means that Ashley might get only 1 GP anyway. USFSA might prefer sending her to 2 Challengers instead, so other USA ladies with 1 spot could get a 2nd.
Click to expand...

Ashley didn't finish top 12 at Worlds (she chose not to go), she's not in the top 24 on the World Standing list (#33) and she's not in the top 24 on the Season's Best list (#38), so she's not guaranteed even 1 GP. After her public hissy fit at her Nationals placement, then turning down 4CC and Worlds, I doubt she'll get a host pick to SA, especially since she withdrew from the last SA. She might get a 6th round-anyone else invitation or a later vacancy replacement.
But if Ashley truly doesn't want to do the GP, none of this would have mattered, as she would have instructed USFS not to put her on the GP selection list.

If Challengers is what she wants to do this season, the problem with NOT doing the GP and only doing Challenger events is incurring problems with the ISU. She would only be able to do Challengers that don't occur at the same time as GP events, i.e. the pre-GP and post-GP Challengers. The pre-GP Challengers (US and Canadian events, Nepela, Finlandia, Nebelhorn) usually draw elite skaters preparing for the GP, and there's only one post-GP Challenger (Golden Spin). To do a pre-GP Challenger, Ashley would have to be in great competition shape early on, and that hasn't been her history, as she hasn't done summer comps in more than 10 years, and the only early-season event she's done was Japan Open, where she was far from her best.

The question is would USFS be willing to send Ashley to two Challenge events if she declined to participate in summer competitions? I have my doubts.
