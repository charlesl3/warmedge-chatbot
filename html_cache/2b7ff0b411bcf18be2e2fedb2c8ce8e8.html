So glad you enjoyed it! I liked Barnum before I'd seen that video, but I finally really got it when I saw it from the right side.

Bolero is the iconic program, of course, but Mack and Mabel, the 1984 Paso Doble OSP, and a really charming "Puttin' on the Ritz" exhibition from the early 1980s (as well as the 1994 OD rumba) are all favorites of mine. And if you think the blade work and speed in Barnum are amazing, wait until you see their "Fast Tap" exhibition (I think it's from 1982). Quillerpen's YouTube channel has all these and more.
