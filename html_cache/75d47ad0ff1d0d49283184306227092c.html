Here's mine:

1. Alexei Yagudin's FS at 2002 Olympics (Winter) --> the best men FS in Olys and that footwork for ages!
2. Shizuka Arakawa's FS at 2004 Worlds (Turandot) --> perfect combination of grace and power and her signature Ina Bauer
3. Daisuke Takahashi's SP at 2008 4CC (Swan Lake) --> it was more like a rock concert with all them screaming fans
4. Mao Asada's FS at 2010 Olympics (Bells of Moscow) --> gives me goosebumps each and every time
5. Jason Brown's FS at 2014 US Nationals (Riverdance) --> the best Riverdance ever; he was a joy to watch
6. Akiko Suzuki's FS at 2013 Japanese Nationals (Phantom of the Opera) --> I could watch this a million times and still feel goosies
7. Yuna Kim's FS at 2013 Worlds (Les Miserables) --> she wouldn't be called a queen for nothing
8. Yulia Lipnitskaya's FS at 2014 Olympics Team Event (Schindler's List) --> the perfect program for this talented girl
9. Davis and White's Bollywood OD --> I learned to appreciate Indian music because of them. I love everything about this program.
10. Yuzuru Hanyu's 2015 GPF performance (Chopin and Seimei) --> one word: perfection
