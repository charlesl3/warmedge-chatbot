US Ice Dancer Alex Benoit of Pogrebinsky/Benoit retires

As the season comes to a close, I have been given the opportunity to look upon everything I have accomplished with Ellie, as well as personal accomplishments for myself.

I've travelled the world, walking the Great Wall, touring the Kremlin, lunching at the Eiffel Tower. I've seen so many things that have touched my heart. It is experiences like this that allow me to discover who I truly am, what I want in life, and what I hold important in my soul.

I've also been so fortunate to represent a country and a sport that mean so much to me. It has been an equally humbling and rewarding experience, the hardest I've ever worked, and something that has taught me so much about myself.

All the while I have had the support of my loving family, my incredible partner Ellie, and a truly remarkable coaching staff. They've pushed me, picked me up, and helped me to become a better, more complete version of myself.

More than anything, I've always followed my heart.

Antoine de Saint-Exupéry once wrote in "The Little Prince":

"It is only with the heart that one can see rightly. What is essential is invisible to the eye".

I think it is time, again, that I follow where my heart is taking me.

This being said, I announce my retirement from competitive figure skating.

I have unending gratitude to US Figure Skating, my friends and family, our fans both domestically and internationally, my club: Skokie Valley Skating Club, my singles coach Candy Brown, the incredible dance coaches (Igor, Adrienne, Greg, Fab) and support staff (Aaron, Veronique, Oleg, Michael, Susan & Steve) of Team Novi and more than anything, to Ellie. She has been the best partner -- both on and off the ice, and I wish nothing but the best for her.

This journey has shaped who I am, and who I will become.

Figure skating will always be a part of my life. Through one way or another, I am certain of this. I look forward to the multitude of opportunities before me to stay involved in this sport, which has become so much a part of me.

I now look to the horizon with ecstasy, ready to move forward as a student and a performer. Sure of the choice that I have made. Inspired to delve into my art, explore the world, my craft, and my soul.

Excited for a new chapter to begin!

Alex
