Hi all,

I'm wondering if anyone knows of any reputable shops where I could get Risport (preferable) or Edea boots fitted in the Bay Area, CA? I have searched a lot over the last few years, but I can only ever seem to find Jackson, Riedell, Harlick, etc dealers in the Bay Area.

I'm asking because I usually buy my skates when I'm visiting my relatives in the US, rather than back home in Australia, in order to get the highest quality fitting and setup. In the past, I have gone to the fantastic bootfitters in the LA area, but it would be far more convenient if I could just get fitted in the Bay Area, where all my relatives live and I don't have to pay accommodation fees

I'm in the area and I've seen exactly one Risport in my time here. Tons of skaters where my daughter trains in Edeas, but as far as pro shops/fitters none that I'm aware of in the Bay Area. I traded emails with Brett Slavensky who manages the shop awhile ago, very responsive and enthusiastic about Edeas. I'll try to post a link, but am usually not successful.

You've got to go with what fits you. My skater was told by her coach to visit Harlick and Sp-Teri, so we did. George Spiteri fitted her and said she didn't need his boots just yet, her Jackson Debut Fusions were perfect, but the wrong width. He said she needed a wider boot so he fit her in that instead. When she gets to upper doubles he said maybe, but she's always fit perfectly in Jacksons. I breathed a bit better walking out not spending $1,000 on a custom (which he said she'd need in his boots) for a 10 year old just yet.

I know one coach at our rink that skates in Risport, I can ask next time where she got hers, but they are so old looking she probably got them somewhere long ago and far away. Only other pair I've seen was at our pro shop being fitted with blades. I know our shop doesn't sell them so the customer maybe got them on line and had the blade mounted. I know our fitter wouldn't touch the boots for molding or punching since they were bought elsewhere and she just doesn't believe in working on those not bought from her. She's a bit prickly, but a great fitter and blade tech so we put up with it.

Best of luck.

If you really want to stay in Risport you may want to contact one of them. Jerry's would probably be the easiest since they are well known in skating. Good luck!
