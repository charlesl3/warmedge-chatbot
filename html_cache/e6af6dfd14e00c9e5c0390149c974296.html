Adding some that I don't recall seeing as yet:

- Evgeni Plushenko's St Petersburg 300 (2003 GPF version) - This is actually, for me, his best-ever performance - and, to utter a blasphemy, I actually prefer it to Nijinsky!

- Max Aaron's Tron: Legacy (2013 Nationals version) - One of the most unique, individual programs, and certainly the best jumping performance by a US man within this quadrennial/last quadrennial. A lighter, more carefree Max, before the attacks really began.

- Jason Brown's Liebestraume (2013 Junior Worlds version) - Still my favourite LP of his, though perhaps his new one might supplant it. Elegant, graceful, and for a long time, his highest TES for the LP. If he had not turned out of the combination in the SP, could he have won? How close would it have been? We'll never know.

- Ilia Kulik's Revolutions (1998 Olympic version) - My favourite Ilia program, and quite out-of-the-box. A throwback of the days of stupidity when the men were required to do a double Axel and could thus use the triple Axel as their solo triple or combination triple.

- Alexander Abt's Rach 2 (2002 Euros version) - Summed up basically as #sashawuzrobbed, or, in the words of Professor Mishin: "a shining star born too early or too late".

- Alexei Yagudin's Gladiator (2001 Worlds version) - I'm really, really surprised I haven't seen this one yet? Surely someone has already suggested it? I thought this was considered Alexei's signature program, even above Man in the Iron Mask.

So surprised that nobody mentions this:
Berezhnaya/Sikharulidze 2001 LP "City Light"
My all time favorite pairs program. Intricate choreography with incredibly detailed transitions. The choreography is so much ahead of its time, and I would say it is still a textbook choreography under COP discipline now.

Some other less-mentioned programs in my list:
-Pairs
Pang/Tong 2012 LP "Scheharazade"
They only skated this LP once, and despite the technical errors, this program is full of lovely transitions and details. The most complicated choreographed program of their career.

Dube/Davidson 2010 LP "The Way We Were"
I will always miss this couple for this program.

-Ladies
Caro 2014 SP "Humoresque"
I saw it live in COC, and it commanded my attention from start to finish.

Mao 2012 SP "Scheharazade"
Different interpretation of Scheharazade with some light and airy feelings. A special program during her toughest moments.

Chen Lu 1996 SP "Spring Breeze"
Another classic Chinese style program

Men
Daisuke Takahashi 2010 SP "Eye"
It makes me wanna dance with him.

Daisuke Takahashi 2008 SP "Hip-hop Swan Lake"
Jaw-dropping.

Jeremy Abbott 2011 LP "Life is Beautiful"
He brings the story so well. Emotional and touching.

Dance
Gilles/Poirier 2012 FD "Imagination"
Full of originality and unique moves, so much fun to watch.

Dominina Shabalin 2008 FD "Masquerade"
My favorite interpretation of Masquerade, better than V/T's and Mao's.
