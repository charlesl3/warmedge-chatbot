I did tell you to go to Al’s to begin with!
I think Everglides have attracted you by promising a bonanza of boots for you to try, but the reality is that if you fit Risport well then your options are pretty much Risport and Jackson, and each will have about 2 models at an appropriate stiffness, this means at the most 4 different boots unless you try on others from other brands to have a feel of them. It’s normal if you to a good fitter for them to only bring out around 2 different boots as they will look at your feet and your level and bring out what is most appropriate, unless you’ve been having problems and need to try on some different brands. It sounds like you really want to try on the royal pro which isn’t a bad idea if you can find somewhere that has them, but ultimately if Risport are good for you you would be fine in the rf3 pros.
Many people drive a long way to get to a good skate tech - and that’s the important thing, a good skate tech.
I guess you (and your mum) will have to weigh up the 5h drive vs the hotel cost you can’t get back.

ETA: I think you said I another thread that you’d already had a virtual fitting with Everglides? In which case they should have little more to add other than getting you to put your feet in some boots, and it sounds like they are suggesting some inappropriate ones and may not have everything you want

Vladik said:
The CA has a short tail? I never even thought about tails before this. Any idea how it’d compare to an Edea Rotation blade (my current) or another beginner blade. Super excited to get my hands on a pair of CA’s though! Just not sure how the transition will be!!!
Click to expand...
Yes!
It's definetly shorter than my old Mark IV (Mark's 9 1/3, CA 9'), even when taking count of the different rocker.
I was very surprised when I compared CA and my Mark IV (unfortunately didn't take a picture), the tail is at least 1 cm shorter, even though the heel plates were exactly next to each other.
And funnily, the toe picks on the Mark IV were of a similar size - would even dare to say a bit larger, but then again it was quarter size bigger as well - and the drag pick seemed to be of the same size. Oh, and the toepick on the Marks are definetly way sharper because of the edges of the picks.

In the beginning I couldn't do anything and even going around the rink was hard, because I felt constantly like falling backwards (yes, I did fall back once...).
But after that initial awkwardness, it became so much better! Holding edges is easier, turns are better and sharper, and going backwards is so easy now, because my toe picks aren't constantly scraping the ice.
And those blades are fast! Way faster than beginner ones.

From what I have encountered with my friends at the rink, the tail of the CA is a bit shorter than that of MK Pro, and apparently the CA is a bit better of a freestyle blade suited for dance because of that. That is just their opinion though

Really? That's odd.

When buying my RF3 Pros, I initially wanted to go with 9'25 Coro Aces as well (because the shop I went to had only that size left), but after asking @Ic3Rabbit and @WednesdayMarch, they both strongly advised me to get the 9'.
Ulitmately, your boots should be measured when buying them, and the right blade size is then based on the in-store-measurement.

Ultimately, I wouldn't worry too much, maybe he was tired or meant to say 9. Just say something about your concerns during your fitting, and ask them to measure out the boots you'll end up getting.
