The year is almost over, I hope you all had a great 2017 skating year. What were your personal achievements on the ice for the year and what are your goals for 2018?

My 2017:

- Got my first boots

- Upright spin, upright backspin (a bit of hit and miss with both though, inconsistent)
- Can do spiral, spread eagle, lunges, toe steps

2018 goals:

- Land a loop
- Learn flip and lutz, maybe axel?
- Learn sit and camel spin
- Master twizzles, cross rolls
- Learn rest of turns rockers, counters, choctaws, loops
- Learn and master Ina Bauer
- Pass Prelim test

We'll see where we are at the end of next year. Happy New Year and happy skating all!

2018
Continue and improve working on sal and toe, brackets, consistent 1 foot spin with more than 4 revs and overall presence on the ice (I look like a Magikarp 85% percent of the time)
Get consistent good figure skating classes (will come true in March, till then I'm surviving with small courses whenever I'm able to, and that's not often)

I wonder if you have tried different entries into the spread eagle? I learnt this tip from a workshop at the lake placid camp:

Say, you are comfortable with skating forward on your right foot, and opening your left hip into the spread eagle. That is, you plant your right hip and open the left hip, which probably means your left hip is more open than your right. To do the spread eagle in the other direction, you skate backward on your right foot, and open your left hip into the spread eagle. This way, you are still planting your right hip and opening the left. So, you can go both directions, making use of your more open left hip.

Let me know if this is of any help.
