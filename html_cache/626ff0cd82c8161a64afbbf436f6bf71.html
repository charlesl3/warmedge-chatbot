A few more I haven't seen mentioned yet:

Alexei Yagudin - Revolutionary Etude (2001 Worlds SP) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=og59QN4L_ko - I happen to think this is his best performance ever
Stephane Lambiel - Four Seasons (2006 Worlds LP) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F893u_lTNVw - a lovely soft touch to this very classical music, and the last minute is just pure joy to watch
Javier Fernandez - Malaguena (2015 Cup of China SP) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n9JLYumaNaU - my favorite men's short since Yagudin's Winter (hope he goes 100% clean at Euros or Worlds to do this program full justice)
Javier Fernandez - Guys and Dolls (2015 GPF LP) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c81eMGmpYoc - this is the first program in my book where the vocals fully serve the program, and is choreographed and performed as much to the lyrics as to the music itself
Shen/Zhao - Who Wants to Live Forever (2010 Olympics SP) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7dbqksMgGfo (from 4:00) - one of the best pairs' short programs of all time
Shen/Zhao - Romanza (2007 Worlds SP) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e5YwnDdnnCw - another one of the best pairs' short programs of all time
Volosozhar/Trankov - Jesus Christ Superstar (2013 Skate America LP) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dxxLXHL7rcA - lovely thematic touches in this grandiose program performed with pure perfection, and a fully deserved world record
Stolbova/Klimov - Addams Family (2014 Olympics LP) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NU-KfPZOUsk - a real star-making turn
