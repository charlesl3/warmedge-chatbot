Yes. I was a Klingbeil customs skater for a long time and they went out of business and I switched to Jackson Customs for my freestyle boots. I wore Risports for my dance boots. I can't wear harlicks because they hurt my odd shaped feet (wide toebox, high arches, narrow heel). SPTeri was also out of the question for me, I'm not a fan of Edeas either.
My advice is to try Jackson, Risport, and Grafs. And possibly Edea (if you end up liking Edea then that's great, it's just not for me).

I just wanted to add, Klingbeil went bankrupt and their staff is now behind Avanta skating boots, so you may want to look into those too. Good luck!

I was a previous Klingbeil customer that switched over to Jacksons. (My Klingbeils were so old they were built by Bill Klingbeil.)

Originally I wanted Avantas, since they are in the Klingbeil family. I haven't encountered anyone wearing them near me, not to mention the lack of fitters in my area and that the base price was 1K. I also found Avanta's customer service to be a frustrating experience, especially when I called to ask questions and requested a brochure. The lone person I know that has them loves them, but that's not enough to make me switch.

I tried Edeas, Grafs, and Riedells, none of which I liked for a variety of reasons. Eventually, I decided on the Jackson LCF 5400/4400s, since the boot height and backstay are the equivalent of my Klingbeils. This was the most important thing for me, since boots that land higher on the ankle have triggered my peroneal tendinitis.

My next pair will be custom Jacksons (same model + tweaks) but I'm really pleased with how well a stock boot has worked out for me. Heat molding + orthotic insoles have worked out incredibly well in my case.

YMMV of course; I recommend locating a reputable fitter that carries a wider selection of brands if you want to compare other boots against your current pair, especially if you like what you currently have.

If you require a split width or other size customization, I would suggest trying Jackson or SP Teri rather than Graf. I was told by a reputable fitter that my split width feet (A heel & almost C ball, with high instep) would be fine in B width Graf Galazy stock boots because "they can be stretched a lot". Total BS. A year later, they are still sitting in my bedroom with brand new blades on them and I'm out $1200+.
