Hi skaters/skating fans,
I've recently had a competition and I've got another one coming up in about a week
I grew up as a dancer, 10 years of intense training and competitions, I started at the age of 3 and quit at 13
During that time of doing constant shows and competitions, I was so used to performing in front of people that I never got nervous
I am also an actor and have been since the age of 5, so I am constantly doing shows and performances
But for some reason, with figure skating I always get nervous before competing
I don't really know why... skating is my favourite out of all the stuff I do but for some reason whenever it comes to competing I get so nervous and don't skate my best
Something that sounds weird but really helps me is my plushy cat tissue box cover [emoji28] it's pink and I named it Trusova (named after Alexandra Trusova of course) and I always hug that before competing to calm my nerves
If any of you have any strategies you use to stay calm before competing, please let me know as I would really like to overcome my weird fear
Thank you!!

I think that the fact of performing in something other than shoes, plus (at times) unknown ice conditions causes extra anxiety. If you can get some practice ice to get the feel of the ice you'll be competing on, I think that helps a lot.

I also like to dwell on how much I've practiced / rehearsed, I am well-prepared, and I owe it to myself to skate the program I know I can. Does this always work - no. But it sure has helped a lot! I don't get nearly as nervous as I once did.

Advice I have heard or read:
- practice practice practice so that your program is just instinct, you hear the music and go! There's no thinking involved
- ways to practice: run your program to music (obv) as much as possible, but also run program off ice to music, visualize doing your program well, run half your program break then run the other half, run your program without jumps, run your program without spins, etc
- run your program then circle ice a few times to keep heart rate up and build endurance, helps mimic loss of energy that results from just nerves
- do competition simulations where you get on the ice for 5-6 min to warm up, get off and wait on the bench for 5-10 minutes while others skate, get back on and run your program
- practice in your costume
- have a pre-competition routine (off ice warm up and self pep talk etc) that you can practice
- every time you skate have some affirmation mantras you tell yourself, use those mantras during competition
- avoid the other competitors' anxiety as much as possible (eg do your off ice warm up away from the noise, use head phones if that helps you, etc)
- stay warm before competing
- you can try meditation or deep breathing also

This is particularly good advice for both competition and testing. And especially so as you get older since muscles seem to cool down faster and come back to warm-up slower.

For competition simulations, you can warm up for your allotted warm up time, get off the ice, and then pretend you are the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, etc. skater.
