christy said:
I know that Klingbeil doesn't exist, and one of the reasons is because they didn't future proof themselves. Yes there are some young people but there are so few companies and they have small staffs so the skills are limited.

Having tried Aura they aren't fully custom. They claim to be, but they aren't. Yes they make a 3D model and print a shell from that, but that shell isn't the same as the custom boots made by Harlick etc. They provide the shell to the skate tech who attaches a blade, heats and puts on your foot with cling film, then the skate tech does the work to try to make it fit. Aura also only provide a very basic flat insole, like you get from the dollar store, so you have to provide your own if you want any sort of support. My understanding (don't have personal experience) is that Harlick etc made the insoles specific to the buyer's foot so the boots were fully customised and ready to wear.
Click to expand...
That's not really correct.
They 3D print a model of the foot and build the skate around that. The issue is, due to the nature of the material, the boot isnt malluable - its very solid, which is why the heat mould is crucial to get the best fit possible (as a long time happy aura user I can confirm my boots always fit like a glove) and also to make it fit the foot more like a glove. From what I understand from friends who used them, for a while they made boots too big for some people without telling them (as they thought adult skaters wanted more comfort) which if true was an absolutely stupid decision.

Having an extra heat moulding after the boot has been custom made (to the foot shape - which is definitely visually the case for me), doesn't make them non custom.

However this argument is a little bit of a moot point as they no longer sell custom boots after being bought over by John Wilson. The way they shut the company down and then sold it without informing customers has kind of turned me off from them as a company (although since JW now run it that's not really important). Either way I can't get dance boots from aura anymore so will be forced to switch pretty soon anyways.
