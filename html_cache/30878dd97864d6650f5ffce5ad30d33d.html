Apparently the Dutch Waltz is 3/4 tempo around 138 bpm.

Kiss From a Rose by Seal is a little slower than that at 132 bpm but could work.

You might try checking out various ballroom dance websites and searching for slower Viennese Waltzes for ideas of other songs that could work. Here's one: https://www.music4dance.net/song/search?dances=VWZ&filter=&tags=&SearchString=
