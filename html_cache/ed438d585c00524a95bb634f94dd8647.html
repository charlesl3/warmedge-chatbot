Icey said:
An Ina Bauer is a "moves in the field" element in figure skating in which a skater skates on two parallel blades.

A skater does not have to be flexible at all to do an Ina Bauer. A Bauer can be done in upright position.
Click to expand...

The skater does not have to be at all flexible in the back to do an Ina Bauer in an upright position.

It is necessary to have good turnout in the hips, although not quite as much as required for an outside spread eagle.
