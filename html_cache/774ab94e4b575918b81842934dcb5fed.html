The summer is here... and along with warm weather comes warm rinks.

My rink isn't steaming hot, but once I start skating I start to sweat like crazy. I got a couple of camisoles, which is good for keeping the chest and back cool. But for leggings, I have to wear footed tights, and then I put skating pants over the tights, making my legs, crotch and feet sweat like crazy. (I end up with stinky boots afterwards.)

So, I'd like to hear what you wear for summer skating?

Good point about skating socks, I never thought about them. The reason why I've always (religiously) used footed tights is because the thickness and friction of the footed part is just right for my boots. I tried wearing some store-bought knee-highs, but those were too thin and too... slippery?... that my feet would slide around in my skates. My boots are not the perfect fit, but I mitigate some of that with bunga pads and correct socks.

My rink is probably one of the warmest rinks I've seen. During winter months, it's warm enough to skate in a T-shirt and tights and leggings. In summer it borders on sweltering. I've seen skaters practicing in shorts (and tights, probably). I just think that looks weird, but maybe I might have to explore that option too.

cl2 - I think you're very lucky to skate in a consistently warm rink! It's much easier to get the muscles going when it's warm and the ice is a bit softer. My rink is wonderfully warm in the summer but does get cold in the winter.

I see the gamut of skate wear all year 'round.
Me - I run hot all the time and I tend to stick with socks or the Mondor knee highs and wear stretchy activewear pants (like yoga pants) over them. Right now I wear a short sleeve top. I see lots of the younger skaters wear stretchy shorts over tights and tank tops.
Although I see skating dresses worn, I don't see skirts worn much. Sometimes I'll wear a skating skirt over tights and a short sleeve top to change things up.

My rink gets pretty warm during the day (all year round for the most part because California) so if I'm skating during the day, I usually wear one of those tanks from Lululemon that has a built in bra plus long yoga pants, plus mittens (all black haha). I wear the Mondor knee high tights under the pants. My feet definitely sweat more during the day, but the Mondor tights do a pretty good job of wicking the sweat. I think the most important thing for me is to wear sweat wicking gear (and nothing cotton!). At the speed that you're skating, it should help the sweat evaporate if you're wearing clothes made out of sweat-wicking fabric.

The temperature varies at my rink, but it can be very warm in the summer. Since I'm an adult paying for my own skating, I haven't seen the need to buy special "figure skating" clothes, per se. I just skate in normal athletic wear for the most part. On top I wear my sports bra and usually a short sleeved workout top. I have skated in tank tops sometimes, but I really prefer a little more coverage. And then I wear just normal long athletic leggings or pants. I have shortish legs, so my leggings are long enough to come down over the ankle of my boot a bit, just far enough to cover the tops of the laces. And then I just use $2 trouser socks from Walmart. They work great for me so I don't bother to spend more. You said you don't like those, though. I think there may be similar options in different fabrics that might not be as slippery. Another option would be to wear a practice skirt instead of pants, if you really like your tights. That way you wouldn't have two full layers on your legs. I know not a lot of people wear skirts to practice anymore, but it would be cooler.

My rink is a warmer surface, but some of the other sheets in the area are cold, so I've built a summer wardrobe to accommodate them all. Sweat-wicking fabrics are a must-have, obviously. Always keep a sweatshirt on hand, especially during warm-up.

Tops: Not a fan of sleeveless shirts, but a lot of my tops have mesh or low back cutouts to keep me cool. I've sewn a few myself, and these are pretty great. I've also splurged on a couple of the laser-cut tops from Alo Yoga, which work nice as a cover-up but still keep me cool.

Bottoms: High waist leggings, mostly Old Navy brand, some I've sewn. When it gets really hot I'll switch over to seamless leggings, since they are slightly lighter and I don't have to worry about any chafing from seams.

Additionally, I have a couple of unitards, which I've had to bust out because the rink was just too hot.

Unlike other folks here, I don't wear socks at all while skating in summer. Yes, my feet sweat, but I use a spray plus sneaker balls to keep odor at bay.
