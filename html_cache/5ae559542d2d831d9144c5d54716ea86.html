Can anyone explain the actual mechanics of this move? To me, it always looks like the blade is sideways to the direction of motion. But this is impossible, right?
Click to expand...

One blade is traveling forward and the other is traveling backward. The feet have to be turned out 180 degrees, same as a spread eagle. (It's possible to do an inside Ina Bauer on a smallish circle with less than 180 turnout, but not a lot less.)

Meoima said:
I've heard that ina bauer is more about the balance than the flexibility.

Anyways, what about couple ina bauer in pair skating?
Click to expand...

An Ina Bauer is a "moves in the field" element in figure skating in which a skater skates on two parallel blades.

A skater does not have to be flexible at all to do an Ina Bauer. A Bauer can be done in upright position.
