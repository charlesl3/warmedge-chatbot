You can do a Biellman from a Camel position as Julia demonstrates above. So it wouldn't be a matter of if it were a camel or Beillman. Their all catch foot spins except a half Biellman doesn't go above your head and is about head height and without the boot over your head.

The Biellman is just a signature catch foot.
