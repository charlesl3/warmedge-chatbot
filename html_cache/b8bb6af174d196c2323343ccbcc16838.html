Hello,

I am an intermediate skater working on doubles and advanced spins. I am currently in Coronation Ace Lite blades and I’m making a switch. I’ve found out that my blades are awful and are not my level at all. My spin consistency and ability has declined since moving from regular Coronation Ace. I compared them to a possible blade (Matrix Elite) and I found mine are very flat. My jumps are okay, could use more height but sometimes I feel like I have to try too hard to get in the air.

I have several options and have had lots of advice and comments from people with the blades I’m looking at. I kind of want to move away from JW. My coach doesn’t have any advice because she’s only had JW.

I’m set on an 8ft rocker but I have decided on these:
Ultima Matrix Elite (straight cut pick)
Ultima Matrix Freestyle (cross cut pick)
JW Pattern 99 (straight cut pick)
JW Gold Seal (cross cut, higher stanchion)

I’m not sure what to do about the picks, I’ve heard different things. I have cross cut at the moment and my toe jumps are amazing but my other friends have straight cuts and their jumps are amazing but that might just be bc they are really good skaters.

I am a very strong skater and I feel like I could transition easily but I’m not sure how it’s going to affect my skating.

Thoughts?
