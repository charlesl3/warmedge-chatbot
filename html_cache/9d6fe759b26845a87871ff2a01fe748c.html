Just jumped on these forums recently. They seem to be the only active ones I can find. Which is unfortunate because Id love to talk to fellow enthusiasts. I'm 25 years old and I used to skate as a kid. I really enjoyed it back then and admittedly, I regret getting out of it. But now as an adult I've decided to jump back into it. Which I'll admit, it's been a bit daunting. My first lessons actually start very soon and I wondered if any of you had any tips for getting back into it? My number one tip: it doesn't matter how good you were as a kid, if you go barreling back onto the ice at full speed, you are going to bite it. Hard.

Take it easy. A lot will have changed since the last time you skated, both physically and mentally.

And welcome to Adult Skating.

Congratulations on getting back into it. I had a severe knee injury day 3 back on the ice as an adult, so I totally agree with karne on taking it easy. From my own experience, make sure you get really solid with your basics first - you may be surprised by what is no longer second nature. I'm oddly having trouble with stopping, so I'm making it a priority to get solid with that first before I do anything else.

My advice is to take your time and don't rush into advancing your skills too quickly, it's been quite some time since you've been on the ice.
Also, make sure that your boots fit properly (this is, if are using ones from the past) because our feet change over time and I would hate for you to have an injury.
Have a fun time!

My best advice to you as the only adult skater in the club is to build good relationships with at least one coach. Last year I too was the only adult in my club and the thing that kept me sane was that one of the student coaches and I clicked. She was the only person who reached out, treated me like an adult, and didn't seem to be thrown by the fact that I was older than most students. I'm no longer in the level she's teaching but she's still a trusted person I can go to with questions.

This year I brought along a friend so now there's two of us. arty: Maybe you can be the adult pioneer for that club!

And don't let your coach skip a bunch of skills because they think you'll be bored. :hopelessness: Insist on learning it right.

(edited for clarity)

I am in the same boat as you! My daughter dragged me ice skating(she's 5) and I hadn't skated since being a kid. I thought it would just be fun, that I'm too old to skate or get back into it. While at the rink I met a 70 year old lady who skates beautifully! I thought screw it, I'm out of excuses now. I have gone a couple times and I am having a blast. Basically, you got to start with the basics and almost re-learn everything. If you haven't been active you also have to get pretty much all your muscles back, I am finding. Despite this, it is the most fun thing I have done in a long time. I hope you have fun too!
