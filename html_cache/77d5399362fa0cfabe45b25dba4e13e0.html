If Edea's really do fit her the way boots need to fit a skater properly, then I would not go any lower than an Edea Overture (rated 48 stiffness) for your daughter at this point. The Motivo is rated only for basic skills, not the jumps shes doing and will be doing, and are way too soft. Overtures are rated for Single Jumps/Axel. The next up that puts her into doubles is the Chorus (rated 70 stiffness).

Good luck!

Do not be afraid to "go against the grain" of any boot trends at your rink. Edeas are popular for many reasons, but if they don't truly fit your child's feet (they don't work for everyone), why pay a premium for something not suited to your child's body? Whenever you can get to a good boot fitter/pro shop with a lot of different boot options, this would be a great time to get your skater's feet looked at and see what boot options would work currently. The more experienced they are, and the more brands they work with, the better. Boot options can change over time.

My skater's boot brands have changed a few times during different periods of growth. In fact, a less expensive boot option has been one of the most versatile. And some boot choices/styles aren't made in smaller sizes. So due to young age/lighter weight issues, in the past we have gone with less expensive boots/more pricey blade combos. And have been happy with the equipment thus far.

Also, my go-to sharpener skate tech guy doesn't currently sell the line of boots we use, so we have to order those elsewhere, separately. But we buy the blades from him and he mounts them, no problem.

Good luck finding the combo that fits your skater and budget!
