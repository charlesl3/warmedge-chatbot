Hi! So this is my first post on here and I was hoping to get some advice or insight into what may be happening with me/my skates.

I have Riedell Diamond 133 skates with the astra blade (I'm pretty sure that's what it's called), and I've had them for about 6 months but have used them for 3/4 (I took a break due to health issues and getting behind in school). Recently I've been having problems with my ankles being wobbly when I do one foot spins and waltz jumps, and occasionally when I do one foot glides but not very often with the glides. I emailed the lady who fitted me for my skates asking for her opinion on what she thinks is happening, if it's that I need skates with more support, or if my skates have broken down, or if I'm not tying them properly, or if maybe I'm just doing the skill wrong. I'm going in to see her sometime next week and she's going to check how I tie them and give me some advice and potentially a fitting if she thinks it's necessary, but I figured I'd ask here too.

I personally don't think I could have worn them down because I haven't had them for that long and they still feel pretty stiff when I squeeze the ankle part of the boot. I try to tie them pretty tightly, but sometimes I have issues tying them tight because of how bulky the tongue is (my one complaint about the skates lol), but I tie them to the best of my ability.

My question is, what do you think is causing the wobbly ankles? The skates? User error? Am I just not doing the skills correctly? When I was in classes I never had a coach say anything about my form but I still wonder. If it is the skates, should I get new ones? I was planning on getting new ones soonish, as I'm going to start other jumps soon (I'm in Adult 6 for the learn to skate program), but this makes me think that I may need new skates earlier than I had anticipated needing them. Thanks in advance!

You were fitted with a boot that is NOT rated to do that. Are you an adult? If so that's even worse because it's a 40 stiffness which means you already should have not been fit in that. Once you started doing waltz jumps in them they would have started to really break down, I'm concerned if they may have even broken down before that.

The lady that fit you...is she an expert in fitting figure skates? If she is not then you need to find someone that is a professional fitter of figure skates and will measure your foot and take all factors into due consideration for your skill level and a boot that will allow you to safely do your current skills, as well as progress, and on or near your budget.

Again, without knowing you personally and being able to see the factors for myself with my own eyes and only going on your info, the above is my advice.

Can you have someone take photos of your boots on you, showing how they fit with you standing in them, including how you lace them, and post them here? Although I do suspect you need a better boot, if there's something up with how you're lacing them, we may be able to tell, which might help you get through until you get in with the new skate pro. In the meantime, this might help:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VCPx7glZIY

This is the method recommended by my skate pro, who fits Olympians; and it's the method I use myself. When she says "tight", I mean snug, but not so tight you're in pain or cutting off your circulation or anything like that. But there should be no slack to the laces.
