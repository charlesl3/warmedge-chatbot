Quick tip that seems to help a lot of the people I teach who are stuck on back counters. A mini edge pull and counter rotation have to happen at the same followed by an arm switch and moving yoru weight to be over the back part of the blade at the same time. Back counters are very timing heavy, so if they're inconsistent, focus on the timing of the successful ones and try to mimic it.
