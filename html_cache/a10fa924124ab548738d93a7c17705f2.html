The illusion does count as a difficult entry, although personally I hate it so I hope it goes next year! I don't mind it so much on a backward entry but I hate that so many ladies do the forward one into the LSp.

I believe that a lunge(or 'drag' in this part of the world!) will also count as long as it is directly before the spin but I'm not convinced about the spread eagle Fedichkina is using. She has four additional features which leads me to believe they don't expect it to get a difficult entry either and it's just a transition.

The rocker-counter entry is difficult, as are the twizzles or any other difficult turns leading into the spin but 3 turns I'm not so sure about. I have heard it said that they will count if they are quick/whippy enough but I'm still looking for clarification on this.
