Hello!

So now that my skating is going splendidly, it was recommended by my coach to potentially take a few ballet lessons. Is this generally helpful while skating? I've done a bit of research and it does seem that it really helps with your movements and gracefulness (Something that can be difficult to come by as a guy haha.) I'm just wondering if anyone else has any experience with this or opinions to offer? Will it benefit me in the end enough that it's worth it? I want to drastically improve my skating as much as possible.

I echo that ballet is important for your lines. I would caution against jazz, speaking from personal experience. Until you get your edges, especially outside edge, figured out, I would stay away from jazz. It's fun, but its emphasis on oppositional body positions does not help your correct lean AT ALL!

Yes posture from a standpoint that if you normally roll your shoulders forward it will help fix the posture issue from it, strengthen your back, learn straighter lines and correct arm placement/movement. It's always good to work muscles you wouldn't normally for skating.
My ds has done it for a few years now along with another dance discipline and it has done wonders. Overtime, you'll notice that your on ice movements change from this is where I need arms/body parts to be for choreo to being a lot more natural and meaningful.

Ballet is the Foundation for many artistic sports. You will gain a better Balance point, stronger posture and flexibility overall and you lose the anxiety to look Bad.

I would recommend every skater to do Ballett and Hip Hop classes. Its also a great Workout if you work in an office.

Look for a good cCoach who has small Group lessons, 5 - 10 people, as then you can get help and wont risk injuries. You could try a local dance school, they will have you covered on various styled.
If you have a Solid fundation, you can go on on your own to lower the costs.

You will learn about muscles you didn't even know they exist
