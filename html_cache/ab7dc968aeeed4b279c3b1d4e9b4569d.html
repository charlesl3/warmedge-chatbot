That is why my daughter doesn't wear over the boot tights or even skating tights. Dance tights tend to be more sheer and fewer seams. Part of the issue is the costume too. A pro costume is usually three layers, dress fabric doubled and a liner. My off the racks costumes only have 2 layers, the custom one by Longmire (3rd hand costume lol) has three. That helps smooth things out too.

Also all skating tights are not equal. And if you have a ring around the middle from the waistband, try going up a size or a different brand. One pair of skate tights we had were so badly made the seam from the belly button to the crotch was incredibly wavy and it was so noticeable I had to run into the pro shop and buy a new pair during a competition. Since then I've just used dance. And if the costume has a sheen to it and not a lot of bling, it will show more flaws too.

Her friends wear skate tights, topped with over the boot tights so they have a seamless look too.

I sincerely apologize for this late response. I'm saying this because i've been wanting to clarify what really bothered me for quite some time, but i literally couldn't bring myself to post anything due to covid and the absence of the Russian skaters. So after reading my email i stumbled across this thread and it instantly reminded me of my most concerning issue regarding figure skating costumes. The severe lack of colored tights.

In my personal opinion, a costume is either a portrayal of a character or the embodiment of the music.
So to illustrate this concept, i'd like to provide a list of colored tights that would look gorgeous with the costumes listed on the right side

Black tights: Les Miserables (You Young WC2022) and Reel Around the Sun. (Sarah Everhardt FC2025) These kind of costumes are always paired with black tights anyways https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nzpAl8dx--o&list=RDMMnzpAl8dx--o&start_radio=1
Blue tights: Ave Maria (Haein Lee WC2021) and International Angel of Peace (Rika Kihira FC2019)
Gray tights: Tosca (Adelina Sotnikova WC2019) and Storm (Haein Lee WC2023)
Purple tights: : Liebestraum (Isabel Levito WC2025) and O Doux printemps d'autrefois (Anna Shcherbakova WC2021)
Pink tights: From my first moment (Kaori Sakamoto WC2019) and Primavera (Olga Mikutina WC2021)
White tights: The Piano (Mariah Bell WC2019), Lux Aeterna (Rina Matsuke FC2025) and Black Swan (Haein Lee WC2021)
Yellow tights: Moonlight Sonata (Elizabet Tursynbaeva WC2019) and The Firebird (Sarah Everhardt FC2025)
Red tights: The Fire Within (Rika Kihira WC2021) and The Phantom of the Opera (Haein Lee WC2023)

I think i forgot to include skaters who have tried this look before. Sadly i couldn't figure out the music they skated to, nevertheless without further ado have a look at some of these costumes.

If the legs are going to be coloured all the way down, I'd prefer catsuits/unitards instead of a dress with skirt plus coloured tights. The latter ensemble always reminds me of little kids in winter, when I was in school and girls had to wear the uniform skirt. On cold days, our mothers would add fleece ski pants for the walk to school, worn under the uniform kilt so as not to crease it.
