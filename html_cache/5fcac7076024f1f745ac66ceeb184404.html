Using a syllable from one of the original offensive names does not solve that problem. If anyone asked where the term "chock" came from you'd just have to refer back to the original etymology, which was the problem the change is intended to solve.

The idea is to base the new names on the shapes they make on the ice.

So if you want to avoid being specific to a particular alphabet, maybe something like "curve step" and "change-curve step." But that doesn't include the fact that these steps also include turning the body. But "curve turn step" and "change-curve turn step" get more unwieldy.

So, I am Ojibwa or more correctly Anishinaabe on my dad’s side. Due to him being 1/2 white, and his parents decisions, he had little connection to his roots and I have even less. I don’t belong to a tribe officially or know if any skater or persons connected to a tribe would be offended by this so take my words with a grain of salt.

If noticing a connection to their traditional steps (as was described in the previous posters article) was the reason for the name, if anything it might be more inclusive. I wish my dad were alive to ask him. He suffered a great deal as a result of racism. Lost legal rights to own and inherit his father’s farm and land since his mother was white and his father legally couldn’t own it either. Despite being a citizen which meant giving up tribal affiliation. “Stolen twice”

What stung a bit was when my girls were in a group number skating to “We’re Coming to America” by Neil Diamond. and some of the Disney stuff. What’s more important is to show respect and maybe educate yourself and others. Not just cancel everything out. I’d love it if their skate instructor would have explained how the steps got their names. Might have been a source of pride instead of something abstract. It seems as if this change is also is prejudiced in that it assumes an attitude for all First Nations people. I reality there’s a wide range of opinions within “Indians, First Nations,natives, indigenous groups” about what is offensive.

I need to read more from skate Canada but I feel like indigenous cultures are actually being erased. Changes like these make them invisible. I’m struggling to reconnect in any way so I’m still learning too. I’d love to hear a different opinion.
