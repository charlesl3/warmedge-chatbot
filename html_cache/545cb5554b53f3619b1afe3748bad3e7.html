Wow.. Satoko, I don't really get where she's gong with this.
For Satoko to improve her jump height she needs to pull off a "Mao Asada 2010", when Mao started to re-learn all of her jumps from the ground up, from single jumps, later building to doubles and triples.
Although, it helped her in a way, her flutz and some UR problems weren't quite solved, despite the drastic measures Mao went for.
If Satoko really wants to make it to next Olympics and be competitive, she has to go for big changes.
Although I respect her immensely for her exquisite artistic qualities, and beautiful lines she creates, jumps are meat and potatoes of skating...
She's barely rotating triples now, she underrotates doubles in 3-2-2 combos, she needs a new approach to her training instead of trying to get underrotated 3A, which will just put her even further down the rankings...

There's not going to be a slovenian judge from worlds 2019, who would give her +5 and +4 on her jumps, everytime she competes. More likely than not, she won't be getting more than +2 +3 for any of her jumps if she's not going to improve her height and distance.
