NanaPat said:
I seem to be one of the few people who prefer Kwan's spirals to Cohen's. Cohen (and Lipnitskya) are extremely flexible, but I don't find those extreme postions that attractive. They remind me of some of the positions circus acrobats achieve; I can be awed at their ability to achieve them while simultaneously wishing they wouldn't. I don't actually wince at Cohen's, but I don't think an extreme position is necessarily better than a well-extended and graceful, less-extreme position.
