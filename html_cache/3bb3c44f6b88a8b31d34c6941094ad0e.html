Re: Meaghan, Eric, Vanessa. From my perspective, ongoing current events impacted things considerably. (Although Eric and Meaghan really needed to have a longer chat before the announcement; common courtesy and all. Given her understandable feelings and very public reaction, perhaps this is part of why he didn't speak with her, although that was the only and right thing to do. She doesn't have a stranglehold on him, but they do have a shared history and the loss of future potential income warrants the hard conversations some men - a higher ratio in pairs/ice dance, it seems - are loathe to do.)

Due to ongoing, ever-changing restrictions, openings and other issues - and Canada has had some serious training differences in lockdowns between provinces, from what I understand - you can thank the political reaction to this pandemic for part of what transpired.

Do *not* underestimate how Eric and Meaghan's prospects have changed for the short-term. If Eric and Vanessa have a year or two in them, there are no guarantees that show skating is still lucrative for the rest of 2021 or even 2022. And Eric/Meaghan live in different provinces.

I don't say this lightly. My kid had a successful tryout at a Canadian facility in February 2020 - aaaaaaaaaand then the rest of 2020 happened. So having distance between two skaters (even "only" inter-provincial) can be a huge issue.

The impact the ongoing restrictions are having on young teams getting together and pairing up is serious. We have gone through four years of tryouts and Ghostbusters comes to mind ("I have seen ***** that'll turn you...").

Entirely understanding Meaghan's perspective (as a former skater and parent of a skater), but if Eric wants to be in Montreal and not Oakville? I've been to both cities, and get that, too. Still, the convo that should've happened didn't.

This sport will break your heart. Even if you still manage World titles and Olympic medals.

Edit: turns out there was a recent conversation. Eric has given context to Meaghan's online comments:

https://twitter.com/CBCOlympics/status/1385004528592986112

If he's competing and she isn't, "well, there's that." So, she's not competing, living in a different province, and has a kid, and a skating school with her husband. Vanessa is in town, wants to compete. Those are the general facts. Nuances in a situation - especially in current times - are something else.
