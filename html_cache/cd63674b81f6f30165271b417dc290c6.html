Back to skating after a bad ankle sprain?

I sprained my ankle pretty badly 5 weeks ago and now I'm dying to get back on the ice and being off-ice for this long is driving me mad!

The ankle isn't fully healed yet and there's still some pain when I go to massage and stretch it but I am able to walk without pain. There isn't much swelling either so I guess that's good? I was able to go for a 2km brisk walk and there wasn't much pain at all.

I am planning on going to the rink this Friday and just skate slow laps around the rink - no crossovers or anything that requires a right outside edge. So I'm wondering if there are any precautions I should take?

I was also wondering if wearing ankle guards or compression socks is feasible? Like won't it mess up the "feeling" of the skate?

Any help is appreciated!

First off are you cleared to skate from your doctor?

Secondly and only if you answered yes to the first question .... can you put your skates on and walk around with your guards on without pain?

I’ve broken and sprained both of my ankles several times and the longer you let it heal the better but I also understand that getting back ASAP can be therapeutic if done properly.

Here has been my best success when returning from ankle injuries. Whirlpools,swimming pools or hottubs that have jets of water can really do wonders to loosen up the muscles. I usually put the sore ankle right up by the jets and let the water massage it. It’s probably the best thing and even did more than any physical therapy in most circumstances.

The last thing you want to do is re-injure or slow down your progress so make sure you don’t exceed any doctor recommendations and don’t just push thru the pain. I usually take a Motrin or pain reliever/anti-inflammation pill before doing physical activity while recovering from injury. It can really help. Just be cautious....don’t cause worse injury by overdoing it. Be on guard and be willing to walk away to skate another day.

Have you consulted a physio or orthopedist?

I've sprained ligaments and tendons in both my ankles over time and my orthopedist suggested I rest it a few days, ice it, wear an aircast (when not in skate boots), and then I could start skating some again, because the stiffness of the boot will help support the foot/ankle.

When you first sprained it, could you walk on it at all immediately afterwards, was there a lot of swelling, and are you overweight? (<--just things to consider, no need to answer publicly, lol)

I got a very painful sprain a few months ago. I just barely managed to hobble to my car right away, there was very, very little swelling, and I think half the junior skaters weigh more than I do, so my doctor didn't even want to me to come in unless it got worse. She also told me NOT to rest it too much so the muscles don't weaken and didn't recommend any kind of guards or socks, just some ibuprofen for a week. By 5 weeks, I was about where you are pain-wise.

I would definitely ask a doctor first, but if the circumstances of your original injury are anything like mine, you should be fine. Maybe ask a sports medicine doctor or physical therapist how much skating you can get away with. If you can walk 2 km, I'm guessing you can probably do a lot more than just slow laps.

I am a physical therapist and I sprained my ankle 2 years ago doing an off ice axel. It was a grade II sprain. I first started skating again 7 weeks after the injury. I started with just stroking and basic moves, on the ice for no longer than 45 minutes. Then after about a week I started doing spins again (it was my right foot I sprained). After about 2 weeks I started jumping and it was a month before I started working on the axel again.

My biggest piece of advice is to do less than you think you can do. Be very conservative getting back for the first time and listen to your ankle. If it starts to feel tired or achy, stop.

I didn't wear any sort of brace but I either wore a compression sock or taped it for the first few weeks.

Good luck.
