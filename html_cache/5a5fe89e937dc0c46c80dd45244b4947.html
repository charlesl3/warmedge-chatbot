I'll put more detail down below, but my basic question is: as an adult skater around 170 lbs, how much stiffer than the recommended stiffness for my skill level do my boots need to be in order to not break down too soon?

I started skating last January and after starting with some beginner skates, got a pair of Risport RF3's in April 2017. The RF3's are graded for jumps through beginning doubles. I only started working on jumps at all in May 2017. I'm now working on my loop and flip. My coach first noticed that my boot on my landing foot was starting to break down in April 2018. So at that point, I had only had the boots for a year. And now, in July, they are still okay for support, but I can tell they're getting worse, and I will need to start looking at new boots in the not-too-distant future. I know that at my weight, I put more stress on boots than the younger skaters do. But I was surprised that boots that supposedly can support doubles would break down so fast when I don't even have all my singles. Especially since it's only in the last few months that I have started to get any good height on my jumps. Oh, and for reference I skate an average of 3-4 hours a week, sometimes slightly less or slightly more, but that's a good estimate.

But it is what it is, so now I want to make sure I choose the right boots for next time, because it's a big hit to my budget and things are a bit tight right now. So I would really like my next boots to last at least a couple years. I like the fit of my Risports, so I would like to stick with that brand if I can. Of course, since I bought these boots, Risport has completely revamped their lineup.

My old RF3 boots have a stiffness rating of 45
The new RF3 Pro boots have a stiffness of 60
The Royal Pro has a stiffness of 65
The RF1 and Royal Elite boots both have a stiffness of 90.

They no longer make the RF2, so there's nothing in the middle of those two options. It's either 60-65 or 90. Both of those are stiffer than what I currently have, but I don't know if I need to go all the way up or if the increase from 45-65 would be sufficient. Not insignificantly, there is about a $250 price difference between the two levels. So I'd like to go with the lower boot if I can. But saving money now isn't really worth it if I'm going to need another pair in a year. But the higher level boots are graded for triples and quads, and even with the weight difference, that seems too stiff for my current skill level. If anyone has any thoughts on this, I'd really appreciate it.

My personal opinion would be to go up to the 65 if you plan to have the boots long enough to work on harder jumps than what you are doing now.

Stiffer boots don't necessarily last longer. My 85 stiffness Jackson's didn't show any outer signs of breakdown and are still hard as rocks, but they became compressed on the inside and started slipping in the heels, and using them any longer was like asking for an injury. I got them to 4 years, but that was really pushing my luck.

Sometimes boots can be "re-built" by the maker, which basically means adding new padding, new tongue, and sometimes new outsole. It can save some money temporarily (should be at least 6 months, but I know 2 folks who have been in re-built Harlicks for several years) . I don't know about the boots you have, but Jackson quoted me $100 to do all those things when I asked a couple years ago for my old Elite Plus boots. I don't know if the companies who have changed to synthetic boots are able to do re-builds on those new models.... I would like to find out, because my new boots were $900 which I probably won't be able to afford again for a decade.

I’d go and try on the higher level boots for triples and get those if you feel like you can bend in them. Go for the stiffest ones that still feel comfortable, you’ve been skating long enough such that you’ll know.

Especially for adults who are used to jumping a lot, as long as your regular skating isn’t negatively impacted by the stiffness, you can go a few levels above what they recommend. I am boots rated for triples/quads, doing doubles but it’s not like they’re any higher than singles!!

I want to say to go for the Royal Pro that's a 65 stiffness because it might be just enough and will probably fit your price range. Using boots as many times as you say you do a week and doing doubles in them you'll be lucky to get 1 and a half years out of any boots. That's just the way it works.

You can try the RF1 and Royal Elites but they're going to be too stiff and too much for you IMO. I used to wear the RF1 Elites and I do triple triples and am on the ice alot of the day, 5-6 days a week, I go through boots every 9-12 mos and that's with having two pairs and rotating them. The last thing I want is for you to overboot and hurt your ankles or knees and then what good are the boots? Also, you will definitely be out of your price range with these and they'll break down at about the same rate you may get an additional 6 mos or so out of them. At a discount online skate business, you're talking $600 for RF1 Elite and about $650 for the Royal Elite (and that's without a blade). The less stiff Royal Pro (65 stiffness) is about $450.

If you have any further questions feel free to ask or come to my inbox. Good luck!
