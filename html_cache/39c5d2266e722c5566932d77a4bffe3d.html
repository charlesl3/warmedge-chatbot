Good God I hope we see at least one by 2020!

I do think we are going to see a lot of... attempts this season. I would not be surprised if US Nationals sees Mirai, Mariah, Courtney, Gracie and at least one more random out of no where skaters trying it.

I think Courtney actually has a chance, she actually seems to have the height and rotation, but not the control to land it. Mariah Bell has only been working on it less than a year (which is an entire other issue really - I actually think the young ones should be working on it rather than treating it as some sacred object to only be attempted by the most worthy) so I am not willing to write her off yet. Mirai, i think will land one before she retires but I am not sure about the "cleanly more than once" aspect or with any consistency. Gracie...sadly I think might injure herself before she get's it. Maybe Carley will start training it besides her and land it first. I would love to see that!

I do think we may need to accept a few seasons of 3A splat attempts though in order to get the idea out there that this is something that should not be written off in women's skating. If nothing else seeing attempt might help get people out of the mindset that 3Z is the last jump women lean/work on. I am of the mindset that skaters should always be looking to grow and improve themselves. They should always have something to work on and push themselves. I don't think skaters should ever feel done learning. Even if they never get a 3A, it should still be part of what is worked on. If every novice lady in the USA started working on the 3A now, I suspect at least one of them would have one by senior, and it may very well not be who we expect and yes I am sure some would get injured too, but if enough start training it wisely, I do think some could do it.

I think this is very true and very healthy. The problem is I think there is a bit too much of a push to "just try it" when it comes to new moves lately, and that is why I think we will see attempts this season. Rotate and fall has worked for some guys and quads, I fear some coaches will decide to "try it" with ladies 3A.

I don't know how many *should* be trying the 3A, but I do think we might see some skaters and coaches that think some hail Mary 3A attempts are a good idea.

I'd like to see some of the ladies try some 3As in their exhibitions this season. That would give them a chance to try it in a program, and in front of an audience, and out of their home ice comfort zone. It would be after the competition, so less fear of injury. It would still drum up hype and excitement and attention, but would not risk turning the competition into a splat fest. If some of the ladies start landing it consistently in exhibitions, then add it in the program, but not until then.
