Isabella Tobias skated with Deividas STAGNIUNAS from 2011-2014, but because of Lithuania's strict citizenship rules, they could not skate at the 2014 Olympics. Deividas had struggled with injury their entire partnership and he finally retired after 2014 Worlds. Tobias paired with Ilia TKACHENKO in the 2014-2015 season, and they had a very successful partnership, finishing 4th at Euros 2017 and 12th at Worlds 2016 and 2017. They had hoped Ilya could get Israeli citizenship so they could go to the 2018 Olympics, but unfortunately he was turned down and he retired. Subsequently she retired as well. I am sure she had no regrets about this final partnership as it brought the most success she had ever had as a ice dancer, whether or not she ever got to the Olympics.

It is likely that if even if he had received citizenship and they had gone to the Olympics, they would have retired anyway after Worlds this year, as he is 33 and she is 28.
